Week 10 — Reducing Project Duration

This is individual work. Download the conference crashing workbook before the session starts. Write each answer in one to three sentences next to its screenshot.

The conference runs 25 workdays at a direct cost of $70,000, and every day it stays open costs $1,000 in coordinators’ time and the venue hold. Each activity can be done faster at a price; the workbook gives its normal and crash days and costs, and the cost of each day saved. In Smartsheet you crash the schedule yourself and watch total cost fall and then rise; in Colab you check your answer exactly. Predecessors are row numbers: G’s 4, 5 means “after rows 4 and 5, D and E”.

Part 1 · Smartsheet — 35 minutes

  1. Import. + Create → Import → Microsoft Excel, select the workbook. Keep row 1 as headers and Activity as the primary column. Check: nine rows, A to I, and Start Date in row 1 reads 11/02/26. If a date column came in as text, right-click its header → Edit Column Properties → Date.

  2. Turn on dependencies. Right-click any column header → Project Settings → Dependency Settings. Tick Dependencies Enabled and map Start Date, End Date, Duration and Predecessors to the columns of those names. Monday to Friday, no holidays. OK. Check: row 9 ends Fri 4 Dec.

  3. Critical path. Switch to Gantt view, open the Gantt settings (gear icon) and turn on Show Critical Path. Check: A, C, E, G, H and I are highlighted. Screenshot 1.

  4. Cost formulas. Back in Grid view, add a column Direct cost (Text/Number). In row 1 paste the formula below, then right-click the cell → Convert to Column Formula.

    =[Normal cost]@row + [Cost per day]@row * ([Normal days]@row - NETWORKDAYS([Start Date]@row, [End Date]@row))

    Open the Sheet Summary panel (right edge of the sheet) and add four fields, type Text/Number, with these names and formulas:

    Field Formula
    Workdays =NETWORKDAYS(MIN([Start Date]:[Start Date]), MAX([End Date]:[End Date]))
    Direct =SUM([Direct cost]:[Direct cost])
    Indirect =[Workdays]# * 1000
    Total =[Direct]# + [Indirect]#

    Check: Workdays 25, Direct $70,000, Indirect $25,000, Total $95,000.

  5. Crash one day at a time. Shorten a critical activity by one day by editing its Duration (for example C from 6d to 5d). Watch the dates and the four summary fields update, and record each step in a table like the one below. Each time, choose the cheapest way to cut every critical path by one day, never below an activity’s Crash days. When two paths are critical, a shared activity cuts both. Stop when the next day would raise Total, then undo that last day. Screenshot 2: the Gantt at your optimum. Screenshot 3: the Sheet Summary at your optimum.

Answer

  1. Which activity did you shorten first, and why not F, the cheapest activity in the workbook?
  2. At which duration did a second path become critical, and how did that change your choices?
  3. Where is your optimum (workdays, finish date, total cost), and why does the next day not pay?

Part 2 · Colab — 25 minutes

  1. Open. Open the crashing notebook, choose File → Save a copy in Drive, then Runtime → Run all. If Colab asks, choose Run anyway. Section 0 loads the same workbook; check it shows the nine activities.
  2. Compare. Section 2 is the chapter’s procedure, one day at a time; section 3 checks every step with an exact optimisation; section 4 draws the cost-duration graph and names the optimum; section 5 prices crashing everything. Screenshot 4: the graph and the optimum line printed below it.
  3. Change the cost of a day. In the EDIT cell set INDIRECT_PER_DAY = 700 and run all; then set it to 1300 and run all again. Note the optimum each time.

If Colab is blocked on your network, download the notebook and upload it with File → Upload notebook.

Answer

  1. Does the notebook’s step log match your Smartsheet table? If not, which step differs, and who is right?
  2. Where is the optimum at $700 a day and at $1,300 a day? Name the activity whose cost per day explains each move.
  3. Section 5: why does crashing every activity cost more than not crashing at all?
