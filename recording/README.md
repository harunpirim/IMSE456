# Week 1 recording sources

These files are **iA Presenter** markdown. One file is both the deck and the script.

## The convention

- `---` on its own line starts a new slide.
- Headings and **tab-indented** lines appear **on the slide**.
- Un-indented paragraphs are **spoken text** — they show in the presenter view and never on screen.

So the script lives next to the slide it belongs to, and there is no second document to keep in sync.

## Facts verified 23 Aug 2026

Corrections applied to the Week 1 material after checking primary sources:

- PMBOK 8 — say "released late 2025, general availability January 2026," not "November 2025."
  PMI's standards page says November 2025; the bookstore listing says 13 January 2026.
- PMBOK 8 sustainability — it is the first **standalone** sustainability principle. It was not
  absent from the 7th edition; it sat inside Stewardship.
- Flyvbjerg — rail is +39% and tunnels are +37%. They are separate rows, not one "~39%".
  Source: *How Big Things Get Done* (2023); method from Flyvbjerg (2014), *PMJ* 45(2), 6–19.
- Chapman — two papers, not one. Chapman (2024), *Project Management Journal* 55(6), Sweden only,
  228 road + 96 rail, is where 2.7% / −1.8% come from. Chapman (2025), *IJPM* 43(8) 102787,
  is the 125 rail + 280 road Scandinavian study finding "on cost, over time."
- Shenhar & Dvir — cite the **2007 book** *Reinventing Project Management* for five dimensions.
  The 2001 *Long Range Planning* paper has only four; "impact on the team" was added in 2007.
- Gemino, Horner Reich & Serrador (2021), *PMJ* 52(2), 161–175, n = 477 — confirmed as stated.
- Scrum Guide — November 2020, 13 pages, Product Goal + one commitment per artifact — confirmed.
  The 2025 "Scrum Guide Expansion Pack" is a separate companion, **not** a revision of the Guide.
- ISO 21502:2020 — confirmed, published December 2020. Entered systematic review in late 2025,
  so re-check before re-recording.
